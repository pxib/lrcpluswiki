Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the LRCPlus wiki at lrcplus.wiki and its community accounts, user pages, and discussions. The wiki is operated from Vietnam by its maintainer. It does not describe data handling by the LRCPlus desktop application or other websites linked from the manual.

Information we use

  • Accounts: your email address, username, account identifier, registration and verification information, and authentication records. Supabase Auth processes your password and sign-in sessions to provide account access, verification, and password resets. Your email address is not displayed on public user pages.
  • Public contributions: your username, public account identifier, joined date, user-page introduction, discussion topics, replies, and their timestamps. Staff roles may also be shown publicly. Anyone can read public contributions, and search engines or other visitors may copy them.
  • Moderation: reports, report details and snapshots of reported content, staff actions, and account restrictions. These records support abuse prevention and review and are restricted to authorised staff according to their roles.
  • Technical information: hosting, authentication, and content-delivery providers receive connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser contacts them. They may retain operational and security logs under their own policies.

We use this information to operate the wiki, manage accounts, publish contributions, send account-related emails, and protect the community. You can read the manual without creating an account. Avoid including sensitive information or another person's private information in public posts.

Browser storage

The wiki saves your light or dark theme preference in local storage. Supabase also uses browser storage to keep you signed in and refresh your session. You can sign out or clear this website's browser data; clearing data may remove your saved preference and sign-in session. Unsubmitted discussion text is held in the current page while you write.

Service providers and international processing

The wiki uses GitHub Pages to serve its pages, Supabase for authentication and community data, and jsDelivr to deliver the Supabase browser library. These services may process information outside Vietnam, depending on their infrastructure and the database project's region.

The wiki does not include advertising or analytics tracking scripts. Public content is shared with readers; account and moderation data are used by the maintainer, authorised staff, and providers as needed for the functions described above. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Retention and removal

Account information is kept while your account remains active. Public profiles and contributions remain available until edited or removed; the current community system does not automatically expire them. You can edit your introduction and delete your own replies using the available controls.

Deleting a reply clears its public text and author attribution but leaves its timestamp and a deletion placeholder to preserve the discussion. Private report snapshots and moderation history may remain after public content is removed, for reviewing abuse and staff decisions. Provider logs and backups follow the applicable provider's retention arrangements; removal from the live wiki does not immediately remove every backup or copies made by other people.

Your choices and privacy requests

Under applicable Vietnamese personal data protection law, you may request information about processing, access to or correction of your personal data, deletion, restriction or objection to processing, and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on it. Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions. Withdrawing consent may affect features that need the information to operate.

You can change your email address and password in account settings, and edit your public introduction on your user page. To delete your account yourself, open account settings, select Password and security, and use Delete account. Enter your current password and confirm the permanent deletion.

Account deletion removes your login, profile, and introduction. Existing discussion text remains with its account attribution removed; text that you included in a post may still identify you. If you want your replies' public text removed, delete those replies before deleting your account. Private report snapshots and moderation history may remain as described above. Deletion cannot be undone.

Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection (No. 91/2025/QH15) provides the local legal framework. A privacy request does not limit your right to complain to the competent authority or seek other remedies under applicable law.

Privacy contact

A dedicated privacy contact email is not yet available. We will update this page when a contact address is established. The account, introduction, and reply controls described above are available for managing your information directly.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the wiki's data practices change and revise the date above. Where a change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it before the relevant processing.
