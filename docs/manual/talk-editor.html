Writing a discussion

# article-id selects an article. ## Title {topic-id} starts a topic; optionally append | Open or another status to show a badge. Use ### Author for comments and add one # for each deeper reply: ####, #####, ######, and beyond. Do not skip a level when nesting; you can return to any earlier level. Indentation stops growing after depth four, with deeper replies showing their depth as a label. Blank lines separate paragraphs. Bodies are plain text.

Place [random4mo] on its own line before a comment heading for a repeatable random jump of up to four months. Replace 4 with 1–120 months. Timestamps never go past the current time; once they reach that limit, later comments can share a timestamp.

Japanese translations are optional. Missing topics use English in the preview and saved manual; leave the Japanese tab blank to use English throughout. Translated topics must keep the English article and topic IDs. Your edits stay in each tab when you switch languages. Saving updates the Markdown sources and generated HTML; refresh any manual tabs to see the result. The preview shows fictional timestamps and omits posting controls.
